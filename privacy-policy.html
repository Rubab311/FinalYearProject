Privacy Policy

Ikhwa Quran Institute International

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Ikhwa, an educational and management application developed for Ikhwa Quran Institute International. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use our application.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on your role, we may collect the following information:

  • Full Name
  • Father's Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Residential Address
  • Educational Qualification and Teaching Experience
  • Course Enrollment Information
  • Attendance Records
  • Quiz Results and Learning Progress
  • Account Authentication Information

The application does not provide a feature for users to upload profile pictures or other images.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Create and manage student and teacher accounts.
  • Manage registrations and enrollment requests.
  • Provide educational courses and learning materials.
  • Maintain attendance records.
  • Manage quizzes and results.
  • Track students' learning progress.
  • Send important notifications.
  • Maintain application security and functionality.

4. Data Storage and Security

User information is stored and managed using Google Firebase services configured by the application. We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

5. Third-Party Services

Our application uses Google Firebase services for authentication and data management. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Firebase Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

6. Children's Privacy

Ikhwa is an educational application that may be used by children under the supervision of their parents or guardians. We handle children's information only for educational and account management purposes. Parents or guardians may contact us with questions or requests regarding their children's information.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide educational and account management services.

Users or their parents or guardians may request account deletion and deletion of associated personal information by contacting us at the email address provided below.

Certain information may be retained where required by applicable law.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
